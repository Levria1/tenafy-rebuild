About Tenafy

Big-operator tools,
independent-landlord prices

We started Tenafy because managing property shouldn't mean paying enterprise rates or wrestling a dozen disconnected apps. So we built one.

Our story

Built by people who got tired of the spreadsheet shuffle

Managing a rental portfolio used to mean a leasing app here, an accountant there, a separate signing tool, a booking widget, and a pile of spreadsheets holding it all together. Every month was reconciliation by hand and chasing rent by memory.

Tenafy is the platform we wished existed. One place where a viewing becomes a lead, a lead becomes a signed lease, and a lease quietly invoices, reconciles and reports on itself. Powerful enough for an agency, simple and affordable enough for a landlord with three flats.

What we believe

The principles behind the product

Affordable by default

Powerful software shouldn't be a luxury. We price for the independent landlord first and never lock essentials behind "contact sales."

One place for everything

Disconnected tools create dropped balls. We bring leasing, billing, viewings and AI under a single login so nothing slips.

Honest & transparent

Clear pricing, no hidden fees, no data hostage-taking. Export your data whenever you want, because it's yours.

Automate the boring

Rent reminders, reconciliation, meter carry-overs, AI replies. If a computer can do it, you shouldn't have to.

Works anywhere

Multi-currency and multilingual out of the box, so Tenafy fits how property works in any market, anywhere in the world.
