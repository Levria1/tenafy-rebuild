From the first viewing to the signed lease to the reconciled rent, every step lives in Tenafy, so nothing falls through the cracks (or onto another invoice).

Leasing & e-signatures

Build leases from reusable templates with smart {{VARIABLES}}, send them for legally-binding e-signature, and let the system handle the rest. The landlord signature is auto-stamped, a certificate of completion is attached, and the signed PDF goes out to everyone.

  • Template library with per-property allow-lists
  • Tenant signs online: draw, type or upload
  • Auto-stamped landlord signature + audit trail
  • Pending vs. active lease states with deferred invoicing

TaneChat AI assistant

An AI leasing agent that never clocks off

TaneChat answers prospect questions on WhatsApp and your website around the clock, in whatever language your prospects speak. It only offers genuinely available units, qualifies leads, and logs every conversation into an inquiry you can act on.

  • 24/7 replies on WhatsApp & web, multilingual
  • Surfaces only empty or soon-to-be-vacant units
  • Qualifies before showing, then captures the lead
  • Shared rulebook & FAQs you control
